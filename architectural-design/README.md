# Casa Lumen Architectural Design

A standalone concept design board for Casa Lumen, a modern two-storey, five-bedroom, five-bathroom single-family home.

The page includes:

- Courtyard-focused exterior concept
- Ground and upper floor plans with room areas
- Dimensioned footprints: ground `14.8 x 10.5 m` / `155 m2`, upper `14.8 x 10.0 m` / `148 m2`
- Wide circulation: `2.0 m` clear bedroom hall with direct access to every upper-floor bedroom and suite
- Door swing arcs, window lines, and accessible opening legend on both floor plans
- Kitchen, powder-room, ensuite, and shared-bath fixture layouts
- Site strategy diagram
- Longitudinal section with double-height living room
- North, south, east, and west elevation studies
- Material palette and indicative area schedule
- Responsive layout and print-friendly concept sheets

The page uses a central bedroom hall upstairs, a guest suite opening from the ground-floor circulation spine, and a stepped-back upper floor. Kitchen fixtures include an island, sink, and cooktop. Bathroom fixtures include tubs, shower, vanities, and WCs.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or serve the folder with:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser. Use the ground/upper floor tabs to switch plans and **Print concept** to produce a presentation sheet.

All dimensions are schematic and indicative, not construction documents.
